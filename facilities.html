Facilities

Dharamshala rooms, a hall, and open air above

What the Kendra can offer a visiting family, a pilgrim group or a gathering — and what to confirm before you set out.

Overview

Three parts to the building

Rooms to sleep in, a hall to sit together in, and open air above. Each is offered on request — please confirm before you travel.

Guest rooms

Plain, maintained rooms off the interior corridors, for pilgrims and families.

Hall & corridors

Indoor space for pravachan, community meals, small functions and gatherings.

Terrace & balcony

An open terrace and shaded balcony, used in the morning and after sunset.

Staying here

Rooms for pilgrims and families

Several guest rooms open off the corridors on the inner floors. They are simple rooms — beds, fans, light, and space to put down luggage. Some have wood-panelled walls; all are kept clean between guests.

Rooms suit families breaking a journey at Abu Road, small pilgrim groups, and anyone needing a night's rest before going on to Mount Abu or the temple towns nearby.

  • Beds, fans and lighting in every room
  • Rooms of different sizes — mention how many are travelling
  • Suitable for elders; ask for a ground-floor room if stairs are difficult
  • Vegetarian food only on the premises
Rooms are allotted on request, not booked online. Send a WhatsApp message with your dates and the number of people, and you will be told what is free.

Gathering

The hall and corridors

The common floor holds an indoor hall with corridors running off it. It is the part of the building that fills up — used for pravachan and discourse, for community meals, and for the small functions families hold when they are travelling together.

There is no stage and no fixed seating, which is what makes it useful. Mats go down, the space is arranged to fit the occasion, and it is cleared again afterwards.

  • Open floor — seating arranged to suit the gathering
  • Used for pravachan, bhakti, meals and family functions
  • Connected corridors for overflow and queueing
  • Dates must be confirmed in advance

Open areas

Terrace and balcony

Stairs lead up to an open terrace above the building, looking out over Sani Gaon and the land beyond it. In the hot months it is unusable by afternoon and the best place in the building by evening.

On the floor below, a shaded balcony with potted plants gives somewhere to sit through the day — the quietest corner of the Kendra, and the one most guests end up using.

  • Full open terrace, reached by internal stairs
  • Shaded balcony on the upper floor
  • Used for morning prayers and evening sitting
  • Please keep quiet after evening prayers

Before you arrive

Good to know

Ring ahead

Send a message one or two days before you travel. Say your dates, how many people, and whether you need the hall.

House rules

Vegetarian food only. No intoxicants on the premises. Quiet hours after evening prayers, and footwear left outside the hall.

Getting here

Search the Plus Code HPR6+P7C in Google Maps. It brings you to the gate, not to the edge of the village.

Availability

Tell us your dates and we will tell you what is free

Rooms and the hall are given on request. One message is enough to find out where things stand for the days you are travelling.

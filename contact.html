On the map

Opposite Chacha Museum

The pin sits on the village road facing Chacha Museum. If the map takes you only as far as Sani Gaon, search the Plus Code instead.

Getting to Sani Gaon

Arriving at Abu Road

By train

Abu Road is the nearest railway station, on the Ahmedabad–Delhi line. From the station take an auto or taxi towards Sani Gaon and ask for Chacha Museum.

By road

Abu Road is on the state highway at the foot of Mount Abu. Turn off for Sani Gaon and follow the village road to the museum; the Kendra is directly opposite.

Arriving late

Tell us your likely arrival time when you message. If you reach after dark it helps to have someone expecting you at the gate.

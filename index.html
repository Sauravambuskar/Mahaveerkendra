About the Kendra

Built for the people who pass through

Abu Road sits on the route to Mount Abu and to several Jain tirths across southern Rajasthan. Travellers arrive at all hours, and not all of them have somewhere to put down their bags. The Kendra exists for that simple, practical reason.

Inside the building are rooms for guests, a hall that opens up for gatherings and discourse, and an open terrace that catches the evening air. It is run plainly and without ceremony — clean rooms, quiet corridors, and someone to answer the phone.
